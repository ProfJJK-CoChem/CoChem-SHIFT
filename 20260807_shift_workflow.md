# CoChem-SHIFT: Execution Workflow (2026-08-07)

## Phase 1: Frame Extraction & Setup
1. **Trajectory Ingestion:** SHIFT parses the `cochem_state.h5` tensor for the 298 K AIMD trajectory generated by KINETIC. It subsamples 100 equidistant geometries.
2. **Reference Calculation:** SHIFT dynamically calculates the absolute shielding of TMS ($\sigma = 31.8$ ppm) at the exact same functional (e.g., wB97X-V) and basis set (pcSseg-2) to ensure a perfectly calibrated $\delta$ ppm shift.

## Phase 2: Tensor Calculation (Parallel Dispatch)
1. **HPC Fan-Out:** SHIFT sends the 100 geometry frames to NODE, executing 100 independent GIAO single points simultaneously using local resolution of identity (RIJCOSX).
2. **J-Coupling Extraction:** SHIFT extracts the full Fermi-contact and spin-dipolar coupling terms, calculating precise multiplet splittings ($^3J$, $^4J$).

## Phase 3: Simulation & UX
1. **Dynamic Averaging:** The 100 NMR tensors are analytically averaged. Statistical variance is tracked to highlight fluxional protons.
2. **Interactive UI:** The user interacts with a 1D Plotly NMR spectrum. Clicking a peak dynamically highlights the corresponding nucleus on the 3D Py3Dmol structure.
3. **Experimental Overlay:** The user uploads a `.jdx` file to directly overlay the true experimental spectrum against the theoretical thermal average.
